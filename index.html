The Process

The Grievance Journey

1. Grievance Filed

Citizen submits a detailed complaint with supporting evidence and location data.

2. Smart Assignment

Our system intelligently routes the grievance to the exact relevant department and officer.

3. Action & Processing

4. Resolution Verified

The issue is fixed, and the citizen receives a final resolution report and notification.
