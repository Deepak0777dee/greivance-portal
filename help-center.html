Support & Guidance

Help Center

Find answers, learn the process, and get the support you need to resolve your grievances.

How do I file a grievance?
To file a grievance, navigate to the "File Grievance" page. Fill in your personal details, select the appropriate category and department, provide a detailed description of your issue, and upload any supporting documents. Once submitted, you will receive a unique tracking ID via email.
What categories of grievances can I file?
We accept grievances across nine major categories: Roads & Infrastructure, Water Supply, Electricity, Sanitation & Waste Management, Public Health, Education, Public Transport, Corruption, and Other miscellaneous issues. Each category is routed to the appropriate government department for action.
What documents do I need to attach?
While document uploads are optional, we strongly recommend attaching relevant evidence such as photographs of the issue, official correspondence, receipts, or any documents that help describe your grievance. Accepted formats include PDF, JPG, PNG, WEBP, and DOC files (max 5MB each).
How do I track my grievance status?
Go to the "Track Status" page and enter your unique tracking ID (format: GRV-YYYY-XXXXX). You will see a detailed timeline showing every stage of your grievance — from submission to resolution.
I lost my tracking ID. How can I retrieve it?
Your tracking ID was sent to the email address you provided during filing. Please check your inbox (and spam folder). If you still can't find it, log in to your account to view all your filed grievances, or contact our support team at info@thestackly.com.
How long does it take to resolve a grievance?
The average resolution time is 7 working days, but this varies depending on the nature and complexity of the grievance. Simple issues may be resolved within 24-48 hours, while complex infrastructure projects may take up to 30 days. You will receive updates at every stage.
What happens if my grievance is not resolved within the timeline?
If a grievance exceeds its expected resolution timeline, it is automatically escalated to a senior officer. You will be notified of the escalation. If you are still unsatisfied, you can escalate further by contacting us or filing a follow-up complaint referencing your original tracking ID.
Can I reopen a closed grievance?
Yes. If you feel the resolution was inadequate, you can request reopening within 15 days of closure. Log in to your account, navigate to the resolved grievance, and click "Request Reopen." Alternatively, file a new grievance referencing the previous tracking ID.
Do I need an account to file a grievance?
While you can track a grievance without an account using the tracking ID, creating an account provides benefits: view all your filed grievances, receive in-app notifications, access your dashboard, and manage your profile. Registration is free and takes under 2 minutes.
Is my personal information kept confidential?
Absolutely. We follow strict data protection protocols. Your personal information is encrypted and accessible only to authorized personnel handling your grievance. We do not share your data with any third parties without your consent.
Can I file a grievance anonymously?
While we require basic contact information to process and communicate about your grievance, your identity is kept confidential from the department receiving the complaint. Only authorized administrative staff can view your full details.
Step-by-Step

Grievance Resolution Process

Understanding how your grievance moves through the system — from submission to resolution.

1. Submission

Citizen fills the multi-step form with all required details and supporting documents.

2. Auto-Routing

System automatically categorizes and routes the grievance to the responsible department.

3. Investigation

Department officer reviews, investigates, and takes necessary action on the complaint.

4. Updates & Alerts

Citizen receives real-time updates via SMS, email, and the tracking portal.

5. Resolution

Issue is resolved and marked as closed. Citizen can reopen if unsatisfied within 15 days.

6. Escalation

Unresolved cases are auto-escalated to senior officials for priority attention.
