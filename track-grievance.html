Understanding Your Status

What happens at each stage of your grievance's journey.

01 Submitted

Your grievance is successfully logged into the Stackly database. It is currently awaiting automatic routing.

02 Under Review

The assigned department has acknowledged your complaint and is assessing the validity and scope of the issue.

03 Action In Progress

A field team has been dispatched or administrative action is actively being taken to resolve the problem.

04 Resolved

The issue has been completely fixed. You will receive a final resolution report detailing the actions taken.
